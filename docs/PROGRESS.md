## Complete beta estimate

The playback milestone represents50% of the full beta scope. Restored product
features represent20%, fresh UI/identity15%, updater/packaging10%, and full-beta
acceptance5%. Their final rebuilding/acceptance is deferred until the physical
playback gate, so the present total is approximately29%, rounded to30%.

The first distributed beta must include the complete rebuilt Windows and Android
experience. A proof application is not the beta. Together1.0 and Pocket remain
read-only references. See DEVELOPMENT.md and VALIDATION.md for actual evidence.
